### For each project: DigiLocker · UX4G · Entity Locker · [fourth entry]

**1. Your role.** The actual title/function on *this* project, not your job
title. One line.
> e.g. "Lead product designer, owned end-to-end" / "UX design, worked with a
> team of N" / "Design system lead"

**2. Discipline.** Two to four words naming the kind of work.
> e.g. "Product design · Design systems" / "Service design · IA"

**3. Value proposition.** One sentence: what the product does for whom, or
what you changed about it. This is the line a recruiter reads. Avoid
adjectives; name the thing.
> Weak: "Improved the user experience of a large platform."
> Strong: "Redesigned document retrieval so citizens find a verified document
> without knowing which department issued it."

**4. Timeline + organization.** Years, and which org you did it under.
*(Needed to resolve the NeGD / DIC question below.)*

**5. Is there a case study?** Yes (I'll build it) / Not yet / Never going to be.
